Built for watching

Live TV & full EPG

Browse channels by category, search live, favorite the ones you watch most, and see what's on next before you switch.

Movies & series library

Rich detail pages with cast, synopsis, and ratings; season-by-season episode browsing; resume exactly where you left off.

// faq

App questions

Is M3U TV free?

Yes. M3U TV is free and open source, built with Flutter and available on iOS, Apple TV, Android, Android TV, macOS, Linux, and Windows.

What do I need to use it?

A running m3u editor server. Point the app at your server's URL and credentials and your Live TV, movies, and series library loads right in.

What platforms are supported?

iOS, Apple TV, and macOS via TestFlight beta, Android and Android TV via Google Play, and Linux and Windows via GitHub Releases.

What is AIOStreams support?

M3U TV can connect to an AIOStreams-compatible source alongside your Xtream library, surfacing continue watching, favorites, and popular titles from that source in the same interface.

Does it track my watch progress?

Continue watching is tracked locally, and M3U TV can optionally sync playback to Trakt so your watch history follows you across devices.
