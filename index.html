The m3u suite

A family of open source, self-hosted tools for managing and watching IPTV — from playlist curation to hardware-accelerated stream delivery to the couch. Built in the open, deployed with Docker, and used by a growing community.

LaravelPHPDocker

m3u editor

A full-featured, self-hosted IPTV playlist manager — EPG management with Schedules Direct integration, Xtream API output, .strm file sync, and post-processing hooks for scripts, webhooks, and email.

m3u proxy

A high-performance streaming proxy with automatic failover and a full management API. Restreams directly or transcodes on the fly with GPU-accelerated FFmpeg pipelines.

m3u tv

The cross-platform front end for the suite — a native-feeling player that brings your m3u editor library to Android TV, Apple TV, and mobile with secure credential storage.

Quick answers

Who is Shaun Parkison?

I'm a full stack developer based in Fort Collins, Colorado with over a decade of experience building web, mobile, and TV applications. I created the open source m3u suite — m3u editor, m3u proxy, and m3u tv — tools used by the self-hosted streaming community.

What is the m3u suite?

A family of open source, self-hosted IPTV tools: m3u editor for playlist and EPG management with Xtream API output, m3u proxy for high-performance stream delivery with hardware-accelerated transcoding, and m3u tv, a cross-platform client for Android TV and Apple TV. All three deploy with Docker and are free to use.

What technologies do you specialize in?

Back ends in Laravel, PHP, and Python; front ends in React, Next.js, and Vue.js; mobile and TV apps in Flutter and React Native; infrastructure with Docker, Redis, FFmpeg, and CI/CD pipelines. I'm a certified senior Laravel developer.

Are you available for hire?

Yes. I take on freelance and contract projects — new builds, ongoing development, and codebase rescues — and I'm open to full-time senior full stack roles. I work remotely from Fort Collins, Colorado. Here's how I work, or email me at hello@sparkison.dev.

Can you take over an existing codebase?

Yes. Alongside greenfield builds, I take on existing Laravel, PHP, and JavaScript codebases — auditing them, stabilizing deployments, and shipping new features on a modernized foundation.
